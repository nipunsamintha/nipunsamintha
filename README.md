### 🧭 About Me

I'm **Nipun**, an Enterprise System Architect based in **Sri Lanka 🇱🇰**, designing and shipping multi-branch, real-time business platforms for logistics, finance, HR and retail.

- 🏗️ Architecting **multi-branch courier & logistics systems** with live rider tracking
- 💰 Deep domain expertise in **microfinance, banking, HRIS/payroll** and group accounting
- 🛰️ Building **real-time GPS fleet tracking** with custom TCP device ingestion
- 🤖 Automating operations with **n8n**, WhatsApp Business and POS sync bots
- 🔐 Focused on **Laravel security hardening** and production deployments (cPanel, Ubuntu VPS)
- 💬 Ask me about: multi-branch architecture, payment gateways, accounting sync
